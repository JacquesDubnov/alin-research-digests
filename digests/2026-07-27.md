### Menopausal Hormone Therapy and Comprehensive Postmenopausal Care in Gynecologic Cancer Survivors: A Position Paper from the FIGO Committee on Women at Menopausal Age
- **Authors:** da Silva-Filho et al.
- **Journal:** International Journal of Gynaecology and Obstetrics, 2026
- **PMID:** 42483786
- **DOI:** 10.1002/ijgo.71188
- **Domain:** Query 1 (HRT/MHT + menopause) · Query 3 (non-hormonal/NK3 + menopause)
- **Relevance to ALIN:** FIGO's authoritative position paper on MHT safety and use in gynecologic cancer survivors — a growing population who face treatment-related menopause and often receive no symptom relief due to unfounded concerns.

> "Vasomotor symptoms, genitourinary syndrome of menopause, sexual dysfunction, sleep issues, and long-term risks from estrogen deficiency can seriously impact quality of life. ### Differential Effects of Hormone Therapy Formulations on Parkinson's Disease Risk: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis
- **Authors:** Macedo et al.
- **Journal:** Neurological Sciences, 2026
- **PMID:** 42481816
- **DOI:** 10.1007/s10072-026-09243-6
- **Domain:** Query 1 — HRT/MHT + menopause
- **Relevance to ALIN:** Fills a critical evidence gap on whether different HT formulations (estrogen-only vs. combined; oral vs. transdermal) differ in their relationship with Parkinson's disease risk — important for ALIN's neurological health content and MHT guidance.

> "The relationship between menopausal hormone therapy (HT) and Parkinson's disease (PD) risk remains controversial, with inconsistent findings potentially driven by differences in hormonal formulations. Observational studies evaluating the association between menopausal HT and PD risk were pooled using random-effects meta-analysis."

### The WinPro Trial: Window-of-Opportunity Study of Endocrine Therapy with Micronised Progesterone in ER-Positive Breast Cancer
- **Authors:** Haggstrom et al.
- **Journal:** NPJ Breast Cancer, 2026
- **PMID:** 42477003
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41523-026-01008-w
- **Domain:** Query 4 — menopause/postmenopausal + breast cancer
- **Relevance to ALIN:** Phase 2 RCT evidence that micronized progesterone (MP) added to letrozole did not increase Ki67 proliferation in ER+ breast cancer, and appeared to reduce hot flushes — directly informs ALIN's reassurance messaging around MP safety and its use in women with breast cancer concerns.

> "There was no difference in Ki67 suppression between letrozole (88.2%) versus letrozole + MP (89.2%) (p=0.39). Hot flushes appeared less frequent with letrozole + MP (13.3%) versus letrozole (22.4%) or tamoxifen + MP (20.5%)."

### Influence of Cardiometabolic Status on Cardiovascular Effects of Oral Menopausal Hormone Therapy
- **Authors:** Rossouw et al.
- **Journal:** Obstetrics and Gynecology, 2026
- **PMID:** 42492078
- **DOI:** 10.1097/AOG.0000000000006381
- **Domain:** Query 4 — menopause/postmenopausal + cardiovascular
- **Relevance to ALIN:** WHI secondary analysis stratifying cardiovascular risk from oral MHT by baseline cardiometabolic status — essential nuance for ALIN's MHT risk–benefit conversations, especially for women with metabolic syndrome or diabetes.

> "Secondary analysis of two double-blind placebo-controlled RCTs of conjugated equine estrogens (CEE 0.625 mg/d) or CEE with medroxyprogesterone acetate (MPA 2.5 mg/d) vs. placebo in postmenopausal women aged 50–79. The primary outcome was coronary heart disease (nonfatal MI or CHD death) stratified by cardiometabolic status."

- **Journal:** Current Neurology and Neuroscience Reports, 2026
- **PMID:** 42496783
- **DOI:** 10.1007/s11910-026-01506-1
- **Domain:** Query 4 — menopause/postmenopausal + cognitive/dementia
- **Relevance to ALIN:** Comprehensive review linking menopause to stroke, migraine, epilepsy, multiple sclerosis, Parkinson's disease, and Alzheimer's disease — a high-value educational resource for ALIN's neurological health content.

> "Menopause is a neuroendocrine process with important implications for neurological health. ### Considerations in Alzheimer's Disease in Women
- **Authors:** Just et al.
- **Journal:** Current Neurology and Neuroscience Reports, 2026
- **PMID:** 42490036
- **DOI:** 10.1007/s11910-026-01504-3
- **Domain:** Query 4 — menopause/postmenopausal + dementia
- **Relevance to ALIN:** Focuses specifically on sex differences in Alzheimer's disease with attention to hormonal transitions (including menopause), biomarker trajectories, and APOE ε4 risk — key context for ALIN's brain health and MHT-for-cognition messaging.

> "Women account for nearly two-thirds of individuals with Alzheimer's disease and demonstrate important biological and clinical differences compared with men. ### Vaginal Estrogen Application Techniques for Prevention of Urinary Tract Infection: A Randomized Trial (TAPER Trial)
- **Authors:** Zuo et al.
- **Journal:** Obstetrics and Gynecology, 2026
- **PMID:** 42492951
- **DOI:** 10.1097/AOG.0000000000006376
- **Domain:** Query 5 — vaginal estrogen + menopause
- **Relevance to ALIN:** First RCT comparing periurethral vs. intravaginal estrogen application for recurrent UTI prevention in postmenopausal women — practical clinical evidence directly applicable to ALIN's genitourinary syndrome of menopause (GSM) content.

> "The TAPER (Techniques of Applying Vaginal Estrogen for Prevention of Recurrent Urinary Tract Infections) trial was a single-center, nonblinded, randomized noninferiority trial that compared outcomes after twice-weekly intravaginal estradiol applicator instillation vs. periurethral application in postmenopausal individuals with laboratory-proven recurrent UTI."

### The Osteoporosis Care Cascade in Postmenopausal Women With and Without Cardiovascular Disease
- **Authors:** Choi et al.
- **Journal:** Archives of Osteoporosis, 2026
- **PMID:** 42486952
- **DOI:** 10.1007/s11657-026-01737-2
- **Domain:** Query 4 — postmenopausal + cardiovascular
- **Relevance to ALIN:** Reveals that 71.1% of postmenopausal women with osteoporosis remain undiagnosed and 87.2% untreated — an urgent public health gap ALIN can help address through proactive bone health screening reminders.

> "Among 5,420 postmenopausal women with dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DXA), 71.1% with osteoporosis were undiagnosed and 87.2% untreated; cardiovascular disease history did not narrow these care gaps."
