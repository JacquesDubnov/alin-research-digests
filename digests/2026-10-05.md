**Relevance to ALIN:** This prospective Nurses' Health Study analysis offers nuanced signal for ALIN users asking about hormones and brain health: longer reproductive span was protective, but MHT started 0–10 years post-menopause was associated with faster cognitive decline vs. no MHT — a counterintuitive finding needing further study.

**Relevance to ALIN:** Largest meta-analysis to date on surgical/treatment-induced menopause and heart disease. Critical for ALIN's content around early/premature menopause: younger age at iatrogenic menopause substantially increases CV and all-cause mortality risk.

> *"Pooling 36 studies comprising 2.6 million women, iatrogenic menopause before age 45 was associated with significantly increased cardiovascular mortality (HR 1.48, 95% CI 1.29–1.69) and all-cause mortality; older age at iatrogenic menopause was associated with progressively attenuated risk."*

**DOI:** 10.1097/GME.0000000000002771
**Published:** 2026-10-01
**Domain:** Query 2 — Perimenopause Symptoms and Mental Health

**Relevance to ALIN:** Cross-sectional survey of 1,980 Japanese women (age 45–56) revealing that caregiving for elderly relatives and workplace presenteeism significantly worsen menopausal symptoms, while community social support is protective. > *"In multivariable analysis, caregiving for elderly family members (OR 1.42) and workplace presenteeism (OR 1.67) were independently associated with more severe menopausal symptoms, while higher community social support was significantly protective (OR 0.71), underscoring the role of socioenvironmental factors beyond hormonal biology."*
