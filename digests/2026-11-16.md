### Association of Age at Menopause with Incident Depressive Disorders: A Prospective Cohort Study
- **Authors:** Wu N et al.
- **Journal:** Journal of Affective Disorders, 2026
- **PMID:** 43014431
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jad.2026.122733
- **Domain:** Query 1 (HRT/menopause)
- **Relevance to ALIN:** Provides large-cohort (n=37,754) evidence that earlier menopause independently raises depression risk in a linear dose-response manner — reinforcing ALIN's focus on mood and mental health as central to perimenopause support, not secondary symptoms.

> Each 1-year increase in age at menopause was associated with a 3.5% lower risk of incident depression (HR=0.965); menopause at age 45 conferred 17% higher risk vs. age 50, while menopause at 55 conferred 20% lower risk — the association was not modified by HRT use.
